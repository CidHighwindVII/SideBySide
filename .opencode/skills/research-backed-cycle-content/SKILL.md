# Research-Backed Cycle Content

Turn verified evidence into brief, useful education without overstating individual predictability.
Read `assets/AGENTS.md` and the content requirements in `new-implementation.md`.

## Research Workflow
1. Define the content question: physical experience, mood, energy, sleep, contraception, support, or communication.
2. Search primary studies, systematic reviews, and authoritative clinical/educational sources.
3. Retrieve the actual paper/abstract; verify title, authors, year, DOI/PMID, and URL.
4. Extract design, population, sample size, measurement method, findings, and limitations.
5. Distinguish prospective self-reports from retrospective recall, population associations from individual forecasts, and clinical samples from general populations.
6. Record conflicting/null findings rather than selecting only results supporting a desired phase stereotype.
7. Map each supported claim to a catalog concept or lesson; review EN and PT-PT wording together.

## Evidence Record
For each source record:
- Citation and stable URL/identifier.
- Question studied, population, and methods.
- Supported finding and its limits; label abstract-only retrieval when full text is unavailable.
- Applicable concept/lesson IDs and wording justified by the evidence.
- Retrieval date and unresolved issues.

## Catalog Rules
- The search-entry target includes synonyms; it does not establish evidence for every phase association.
- Separate canonical concepts, search aliases, and reviewed stage applicability.
- Include positive/neutral experiences and concepts with no proven stage relationship.
- Do not fabricate citations, precision, causal links, or uniform experiences to fill the catalog.
- Check existing `Research papers.md` claims against sources; deleted-spec recommendations are not current product authority.

## Micro-Lessons
- One question/headline, two or three short cards, and one practical takeaway in roughly 30–60 seconds.
- Optional one-question challenge; source details and deeper reading remain accessible on tap.
- Use tentative descriptions and direct support advice; do not dismiss legitimate concerns as hormones.
- Preserve stable IDs, explicit completion, and paired EN/PT content.

## Verification and Handoff
Validate translations, alias/source references, IDs, quiz answer bounds, and evidence-to-content coverage.
Report sources actually reviewed, claims changed, limitations, and content still awaiting review.
